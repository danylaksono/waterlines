Stroke-and-erase vs distance field

The same geometry, the same style, the same view - two renderers. Left is the port of Olivia Vane's canvas method: N wide strokes, each with its middle erased. Right computes the distance to the shore once per frame with a jump flood and reads every waterline off it in a single pass. Drag either map; they stay in step. Watch the counters when you raise the line count, and watch which one keeps animating while you drag.
